Why This Problem?

The Problem

Stress is a leading cause of burnout and mental health issues worldwide. Most people don't realise they're heading toward High stress until it's too late.

The Solution

Predict stress level from daily habits — sleep, work hours, screen time, exercise — before it becomes serious.

Target: Stress Level

Low / Moderate / High

Originally planned Mental Health Condition (5 classes) but EDA showed ~20% F1 = random. Stress Level has direct causal link to lifestyle.

Real-World Value

  • HR tools for employee wellness
  • Personal health apps
  • Early intervention systems

EDA → Decisions

Sleep varies across stress levels
→ Engineered sleep_debt = max(0, 8 - sleep_hours)
Work hours higher at High stress
→ Engineered work_intensity = hours / 40
Screen time > 6h linked to High stress
→ Engineered binary screen_overload flag
Classes are balanced (~1000 each)
→ Metric: F1 Macro (weights all classes equally)
Features low-correlated
→ Keep all features — no redundancy

Models: Baseline → Improvement

Baseline: Decision Tree

Single tree, max_depth=5. Simple, visualisable, but high variance — one bad split corrupts everything below it.

0.2819
F1 Macro

Improvement: XGBoost + Optuna

300 trees built sequentially. Each tree corrects the mistakes of the previous one (boosting).

Optuna ran 50 trials to find best hyperparameters.

0.3669
F1 Macro (confirmed by 5-fold CV)

Best hyperparameters found by Optuna:   learning_rate = 0.016   n_estimators = 235   max_depth = 5

Lower learning rate = more careful correction per tree. MLflow tracked every trial.

SHAP: Why Did the Model Predict This?

What is SHAP?

Tells us which features pushed the prediction and by how much. Not just WHAT — but WHY.

Example: 4h sleep, 60h work, no exercise

Class Probability
Low 12%
Moderate 23%
High 65%

Top Drivers for High Stress

  • 🔴 High sleep_debt → toward High stress
  • 🔴 High work_intensity → toward High stress
  • 🔴 screen_overload → toward High stress
  • 🟢 High happiness_score → away from High
  • 🟢 High exercise → away from High

Why This Matters

A model you can't explain is hard to trust. SHAP lets us say: "Change your sleep and screen habits to reduce stress risk."

Production & Live Demo

How it works in production

Real-time: FastAPI endpoint — HR system or mobile app sends lifestyle data, gets prediction back in milliseconds.

Batch: Run predictions nightly on all users, flag anyone moving toward High stress.

What to monitor

  • Prediction drift — if 80% suddenly predict High, something changed
  • Data drift — average sleep hours dropping in new data
  • Model decay — F1 dropping over time as habits change

Tools: Evidently AI, MLflow monitoring
